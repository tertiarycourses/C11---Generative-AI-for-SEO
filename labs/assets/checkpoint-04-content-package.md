## Selected Opening

Start with the trip, not the pouch. Write down why you are travelling, how long you
will be away and which items are essential before choosing an organiser. This guide
then takes you through seven deliberate packing steps and shows where MerlionTrail's
three labelled sample pouch sizes can separate clothing, small accessories and
laundry. [SOURCE: Modular set] It also points to the supplied care sequence and the
written instructions for the replaceable zipper pull. [SOURCE: Care information;
Repair feature] Check current carrier requirements with an authoritative source
before travel because no live carrier rule is supplied here. [EDITOR INPUT NEEDED:
Product Editor to test the sequence and add a first-hand usability note.]

## Drafted Seven-Step Checklist

1. **State the trip purpose and duration.** Write a short trip brief before making a
packing list. Keep any live airline or carrier rule outside this course source pack
until a human checks a current authoritative source. [EDITOR INPUT NEEDED: Product
Editor to confirm which current source was checked.]

2. **List essential items before choosing organisers.** Separate needs from optional
items so the packing decision starts with the traveller's job rather than a product.
The page must not claim a percentage of space or time saved because no performance
evidence is supplied. [SOURCE: Unknowns - Performance]

3. **Group the list into clothing, small accessories and laundry.** These groups match
the intended labels in the synthetic sample organiser set. [SOURCE: Modular set] Do
not infer dimensions, capacity or suitability for an item that the source pack does
not name.

4. **Match each group to one of the three labelled sample pouch sizes.** The source
states that the set has three labelled sizes intended to separate those groups.
[SOURCE: Modular set] A Product Editor must try the sequence before adding advice
about fit or ease of use. [EDITOR INPUT NEEDED: record the editor's observation.]

5. **Keep frequently needed items easy to reach.** Place items according to the
traveller's planned sequence of use. Treat this as practical editorial guidance, not
a product-performance claim. [EDITOR INPUT NEEDED: Product Editor to add one observed
example from a controlled packing review.]

6. **Remove items that do not serve the trip.** Review the list against the trip brief,
then explain any decision to remove or retain an item. Do not turn this step into a
promise that the organiser reduces weight, space or packing time. [SOURCE: Unknowns -
Performance]

7. **Confirm the arrangement and plan for care.** Check any current carrier
requirements with an authoritative source before travel. After use, the supplied care
card says to empty the organiser, spot clean with mild soap and air dry it fully
before storage. [SOURCE: Care information]

### Repair-or-Replace Decision Box

Use the written replacement instructions only when the issue is the replaceable
zipper pull. [SOURCE: Repair feature] For damage to the shell, seams or another
component, hold the repair decision and seek product-specific guidance; the source
pack does not authorise another repair. No environmental benefit is claimed.
[SOURCE: Unknowns - Certification and comparative position]
